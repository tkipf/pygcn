# GCN Ablation Study Summary

## Experimental Setup
- Model: 2-layer GCN
- Dataset: Cora
- Training epochs: 200
- Learning rate: 0.01
- Weight decay: 5e-4
- Hidden units: 16
- Dropout: 0.5

## 1. Self-Loop Ablation Study

### Results:
| Configuration       | Test Accuracy |
|---------------------|---------------|
| With Self-Loop      | 0.812         |
| Without Self-Loop   | 0.776         |

### Analysis:
- Adding self-loop improves test accuracy by 3.6% (0.812 vs 0.776)
- Self-loop helps the model preserve node-specific information
- Without self-loop, nodes can only aggregate information from neighbors, potentially losing important self-features

## 2. Normalization Ablation Study

### Results:
| Configuration           | Test Accuracy |
|-------------------------|---------------|
| Symmetric Normalization | 0.808         |
| Left Normalization      | 0.822         |

### Analysis:
- Left normalization (row-wise) performs slightly better than symmetric normalization
- Accuracy difference: 1.4% (0.822 vs 0.808)
- Left normalization ensures each node's features are properly scaled before aggregation
- Symmetric normalization may lead to over-smoothing in some cases

## Key Takeaways
1. Self-loop is crucial for GCN performance, providing significant accuracy improvement
2. Normalization choice has smaller but noticeable impact on final results
3. The combination of self-loop and left normalization yields the best performance in this study
